We book from the same contracted rates we use inside our holiday packages, which is usually better than the public price for the same room. Where it is not, we will tell you.

Tell us the city, the dates, how many rooms and roughly what you want to spend, and we come back with two or three options rather than a list of two hundred.

Bookings can be held while you check with the rest of the party. Nothing is charged until you confirm, and we never ask for card details at the enquiry stage.

  • Airport transfers added to any booking
